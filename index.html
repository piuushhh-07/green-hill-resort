A Place for Every Celebration

Green Hill Resort was built for the pause between a busy week and the next one — a resort destination where the only schedule is yours. Enjoy time by the swimming pool, make memories with family and friends, or celebrate your special occasions in a relaxed setting.

Enjoy the swimming pool and water slides, celebrate in the banquet hall, or host an occasion on the open-air lawn. Vegetarian and non-vegetarian food options are available.
